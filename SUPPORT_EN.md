# SwiftRename — Support

SwiftRename is a macOS app for renaming, copying, and moving files in batches. Photos and videos are named after their capture date (EXIF) and, if you like, sorted automatically into folders by year, month, or day; alternatively the app numbers files sequentially with your own prefix, or keeps the names and only sorts the files into folders. Duplicates are detected by size and content and skipped.

## Help in the app

SwiftRename ships with a complete built-in manual. Open it from the Help menu or with ⌘/ (Command-Slash). It explains all rename modes, the folder structures, duplicate detection, and the remaining options.

## Frequently asked questions

**My photos don't get their capture date.**
Check that "Use camera date" is enabled. If a file has no EXIF capture date, SwiftRename uses its creation date instead.

**Why were some files skipped?**
Most likely duplicate detection recognized them as already present (same size and same content in the destination folder). The dialog shows both files side by side; you can also turn the check off with "Check for duplicates".

**Can I undo a rename operation?**
Yes — right after the run, press ⌘Z (Edit → Undo). Copied files are moved to the Trash; moved files return to where they came from.

**How do I change the app's language?**
macOS lets you pick a language per app: System Settings → General → Language & Region → "Apps" section. Add SwiftRename there and choose the language you want.
